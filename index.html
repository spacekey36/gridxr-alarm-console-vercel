2. Top 3 Insights for the CFO

Insight 1: Severe Payment Channel Governance Failure – 66.6% (₹3.19 Crore) Disbursed via Informal UPI & Employee Reimbursements

In a heavy construction and infrastructure enterprise, high-value expenditures such as Contract Labour (₹11.54M), Construction Materials (₹7.25M), and Equipment Rentals (₹5.88M) must follow a strict Purchase Order → Goods Receipt Note (GRN) → Accounts Payable 3-way matching process with 30-day vendor credit terms.

Instead, traditional formal Vendor Payments account for ONLY 7.0% of spend (₹3.35M across 64 txs). Meanwhile, 38.1% (₹18.27M) is disbursed over instant UPI, and 28.5% (₹13.62M) is settled via employee personal reimbursement claims. Site coordinators and project managers are paying labour contractors and equipment suppliers out of pocket or via mobile wallets.

CFO Impact: Total loss of working capital credit float, zero withholding tax (TDS) automation at source, risk of lost GST Input Tax Credits (ITC), and immense vulnerability to fictitious labour billing.

Insight 2: "Finance HQ" Chronic Budgetary Overrun (+₹2.82M / +138% to +313% MoM) Caused by Cost Misallocation

While total company budget utilization appears artificially low (19.4% due to large, unspent project paper budgets of ₹246.5M), central Finance HQ - Bengaluru is deeply underwater every single month:

  • Allocated monthly budget: ~₹180K – ₹202K/month.
  • Actual monthly spend: ₹462K – ₹809K/month (over-budget by +138% in June, +157% in Feb, +174% in Jan, +188% in Apr, +236% in Aug, +276% in Mar, and a staggering +313% in July).

Root Cause: Finance executives (Kavya Nair, Pranav Patel, Shreya Sharma, Ananya Singh) are booking project-level capital expenditures—including ₹1.83M to Apex Consulting, ₹819K in heavy Equipment Rentals (PowerGen Rentals), and ₹434K in Construction Materials (BuildMart Supplies)—directly to the corporate Finance HQ cost center instead of allocating them to the respective project site codes (P-Alpha through P-Epsilon).

Insight 3: Widespread Internal Control Overrides & Automated System Flaws (103 Manager Overrides + 15 Auto-Approved Violations)

Automated corporate finance controls flagged 143 transactions (₹13.78M, or 28.8% of spend) as Policy Non-Compliant. However, the approval hierarchy functions as a rubber stamp:

  • 103 non-compliant transactions (₹9.09M) were Approved by line managers anyway (led by Diya Mehta with 14 overrides and Rahul Iyer with 12 overrides).
  • 15 non-compliant transactions (₹1.42M) were Auto-Approved by the ERP/system without human eyes, including ₹265,334 to SiteServe Contractors lacking receipts (T000566) and ₹219,930 to BuildMart Supplies missing approvals (T000394).
  • ₹5.93 Million across 132 transactions has NO receipt attached, yet 78% of these claims were approved and disbursed.

3. One Thing to Investigate Further

Priority Investigation: Intentional Transaction Structuring (Smurfing) by Diya Sharma at Pune Site 1

The Anomaly:
In reviewing transaction logs, we identified 6 transactions submitted by Diya Sharma (E1016, Operations Manager) at Pune Site 1 payable to Metro Hardware via instant UPI for "Urgent site tools purchase":

  • 14-Jul-2026: T000905 (₹49,800.00) + T000906 (₹49,750.00) → Same-day total: ₹99,550.00
  • 22-Aug-2026: T000907 (₹49,800.00) + T000908 (₹49,750.00) → Same-day total: ₹99,550.00
  • 08-Sep-2026: T000909 (₹49,800.00) + T000910 (₹49,750.00) → Same-day total: ₹99,550.00

Why This Demands Formal Investigation:
This is textbook structuring/smurfing. Northstar's operational authorization matrix or UPI single-transaction limit is set at ₹50,000. In each of the three instances, an identical ~₹100,000 purchase was split into two sub-₹50,000 tranches within minutes of each other. Furthermore, all 6 transactions were signed off by the same approver (Nisha Khanna) within a flat 2.0-hour turnaround without raising an alert.

Additional Information Required Before Drawing a Conclusion:

  1. Itemized Invoices & GST Bills from Metro Hardware: Inspect the physical or electronic tax invoices corresponding to DOC-812132, DOC-463784, DOC-342874, DOC-245138, DOC-742577, and DOC-619069 to determine if goods were actually received or if this represents split fictitious invoicing.
  2. Physical Asset Verification at Pune Site 1: Have internal audit conduct a physical inventory check of the tools allegedly procured under these purchase orders.
  3. UPI Beneficiary Bank Account Audit: Verify the VPA (Virtual Payment Address) and bank account details of the Metro Hardware recipient to ensure it is not an employee-linked or proxy account.
  4. Approver Interview (Nisha Khanna): Inquire whether Nisha Khanna was aware that these were simultaneous purchases on the same day and why they were not escalated as a single consolidated capital PO.
  5. Cross-Check with Duplicate Invoices: Cross-reference whether Metro Hardware invoices were also duplicated elsewhere, given that duplicate invoice DOC-165800 (₹89,604.07) was also issued by Metro Hardware to Gaurav Bose.
